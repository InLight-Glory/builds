# Human Manager Perks & Quirks

Each rescued human is generated with a random set of 3 to 7 perks from the list below. As a commander, you have a choice: assign willing volunteers as managers to gain their positive perks, or force the unwilling to work, which comes at a cost.

## The Coercion Mechanic

If a player forces an unwilling survivor to become a manager, that human's morale plummets. This has a direct gameplay effect:

1.  1/3 of their perks (rounded down) are reversed, turning from a positive Perk into a negative Quirk.
2.  A human with 3-5 perks will have 1 Quirk. A human with 6-7 perks will have 2 Quirks.
3.  The specific perks that get reversed into Quirks are chosen randomly.

## Positive Perks (Volunteers)

Assigning a willing human as a manager to a building or a bot provides them with a unique perk based on their background and skills.

### Building Manager Perks

#### Efficiency & Production

* **Logistics Expert:** Increases the resource output or production speed of this building by 15%.
* **Taskmaster:** Increases production speed by 30%, but also increases the building's power consumption by 10%.
* **Recycler:** This building has a 10% chance to refund a portion of the resources used when producing a bot or item.
* **Morale Booster:** (Main Base Only) Increases the efficiency of all other human managers by 5%.

#### Defense & Security

* **Structural Engineer:** Increases this building's maximum health by 30%.
* **Vigilant Sentry:** (Defensive Turrets Only) Increases this turret's targeting range by 25%.
* **Maintenance Chief:** This building slowly regenerates health over time.
* **Blast Plating:** This building takes 20% less damage from explosive enemy types.

#### Noise & Stealth

* **Acoustic Dampening:** Reduces the "Noise" generated by this building and its upgrades by 30%.
* **Low Profile:** This building is less likely to be the initial target of a horde wave.
* **Strategic Downgrade:** The resource cost to downgrade this building is fully refunded.

#### Research & Technology

* **Inspired Scientist:** (Research Lab Only) Has a 15% chance each day for a "breakthrough," instantly completing 25% of remaining research time.
* **Tinkerer:** This building passively generates a small number of research points over time.
* **Modular Designer:** Reduces the resource cost of upgrading this specific building by 20%.

### Bot Manager Perks

#### Efficiency & Gathering

* **Optimized Scrapper:** The managed Scrapper bot has a 10% chance to gather double resources from a single node.
* **Geologist:** The managed Scrapper bot can identify and harvest rare resource nodes.
* **Fleet Footed:** The managed Mule bot moves 20% faster when hauling resources.

#### Combat & Survival

* **Combat Tactician:** The managed combat bot gains a 10% damage bonus.
* **Guardian Protocol:** If the player is downed near the managed bot, it will deploy a temporary defensive shield.
* **Self-Repair Nanites:** The managed bot will slowly regenerate health when out of combat.

#### Stealth & Support

* **Silent Running:** Reduces the "Noise" generated by the managed bot's actions by 50%.
* **Decoy Beacon:** Once per horde wave, the managed bot can deploy a decoy that draws enemy aggression.
* **Extended Fuel Cells:** The managed bot can operate for 25% longer before needing to refuel.

## Negative Quirks (Coerced Managers)

These are the reversed versions of the perks above, activated when a human is forced into service.

### Building Manager Quirks

#### Inefficiency & Sabotage

* **Careless Handler:** Decreases the resource output or production speed of this building by 15%.
* **Unmotivated:** Decreases production speed by 20% and has a chance to randomly halt production for a short time.
* **Wasteful:** This building has a 10% chance to consume extra resources when producing a bot or item.
* **Demoralizer:** (Main Base Only) Decreases the efficiency of all other human managers by 5%.

#### Insecurity & Negligence

* **Shoddy Workmanship:** Decreases this building's maximum health by 20%.
* **Distracted:** (Defensive Turrets Only) Decreases this turret's targeting range by 25%.
* **Negligent:** This building slowly loses health over time due to poor upkeep, requiring frequent repairs.
* **Volatile Materials:** This building takes 20% more damage from explosive enemy types.

#### Noise & Recklessness

* **Loud & Clumsy:** Increases the "Noise" generated by this building and its upgrades by 30%.
* **High Profile:** This building is more likely to be the initial target of a horde wave.
* **Stubborn:** Downgrading this building costs extra resources as the manager resists the change.

#### Research & Stagnation

* **Methodical Mundanity:** (Research Lab Only) Research projects managed by this human can never have a "breakthrough" and have a chance to take longer than estimated.
* **Technophobe:** This building passively drains a small number of research points over time.
* **Over-Complicator:** Increases the resource cost of upgrading this specific building by 20%.

### Bot Manager Quirks

#### Inefficiency & Carelessness

* **Inefficient Scrapper:** The managed Scrapper bot has a 10% chance to gather less resources from a single node.
* **Tunnel Vision:** The managed Scrapper bot cannot see or harvest rare resource nodes.
* **Lead-Footed:** The managed Mule bot moves 20% slower when hauling resources.

#### Combat & Cowardice

* **Hesitant Tactician:** The managed combat bot suffers a 10% damage penalty.
* **Self-Preservation Protocol:** If the player is attacked near the managed bot, it will flee to a safe distance.
* **System Corrosion:** The managed bot slowly loses health over time due to skipped maintenance.

#### Recklessness & Unreliability

* **Noisy Operation:** Increases the "Noise" generated by the managed bot's actions by 50%.
* **Attraction Beacon:** The managed bot periodically attracts nearby enemies to its location.
* **Leaky Fuel Cells:** The managed bot must refuel 25% more often.
