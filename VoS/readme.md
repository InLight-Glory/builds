## The World is Broken. The War is Eternal.

Welcome to Gray-Earth, a world of forgotten wonders. An age of unprecedented harmony, built upon the resonant power of a mysterious material called **The Keystone**, was shattered by a cataclysm known as **The Fracture**. This event cracked reality itself, flooding the world with a toxic, reality-warping energy called the Static Miasma.

Now, the last remnants of civilization huddle in two rival **Sanctuaries**. Believing the other is a corruption that perpetuates the chaos, they are locked in a desperate war for the planet's future.

You are a **Conductor**, your mind linked directly to your Sanctuary's Keystone. From this link, you manifest **Echoes**—perfect physical reconstructions of legendary figures from across time, known as Vessels. But you are trapped. Unbeknownst to all, the war is a self-perpetuating cycle. Each time one Sanctuary achieves victory, the resulting imbalance resets the timeline, and the war begins anew. Your greatest triumph is your ultimate defeat.

Can you uncover the fragmented truths hidden within the loop and find a way to break the cycle?

## ✨ Key Features

* **🌀 The Echo System:** You don't just control one hero—you command a synergistic pair. Instantly swap between your two chosen Vessels with a 5-second cooldown. Dodge lethal blows, create mind-bending combos, and manage two health and energy bars.

* **⚔️ True Action Combat:** No point-and-click. With **WASD movement** and a mouse-driven camera, every move is under your direct control. Land skill shots, dodge attacks, and master a combat system featuring light, heavy, and special ability attacks.

* **🔗 Unified Itemization:** A single item build applies to **both** of your Vessels. This creates incredible strategic depth, forcing you to purchase items that benefit your entire pairing.

* **🗺️ A Living Battlefield:** The jungle is alive with strategic opportunities. Capture asymmetric **Temples** guarded by ever-stronger warriors to buff your team or debuff your enemies. Defeat the monstrous **Overgrowth Darkness** and summon it as a devastating siege ally.

## 🎭 The Vessels

Choose two Vessels from five distinct archetypes, each a legend reborn from the Keystone Datastream.

* **🛡️ Guardians:** Durable front-line protectors who excel at absorbing damage, controlling enemy movement, and defending their allies.
* **✊ Combaters:** Aggressive melee fighters who thrive in the thick of battle, dealing sustained damage and pursuing high-priority targets.
* **📖 Specialists:** Masters of area control and unique tactical abilities who manipulate the battlefield with powerful, often zone-based, skills.
* **🎯 Marksmen:** Ranged damage dealers who excel at attacking from a distance, taking down objectives, and eliminating key enemies with precision strikes.
* **⚡ Enhancers:** Tactical supporters who empower their allies with shields, healing, and other buffs, or disable enemies to turn the tide of a fight.

## 🎮 Game Modes

* ### PvP
    * **Sanctuary Assault (5v5):** The classic MOBA experience. Push lanes, destroy enemy Landmarks, and shatter their Sanctuary. This is the climactic battle of each time loop.
    * **Synergy Skirmish (3v3):** A fast-paced arena deathmatch where the first team to 30 kills wins.

* ### PvE & Single Player
    * **Story Mode (Single Player):** Play through a unique 7-chapter narrative for each Vessel, exploring their backstory and the secrets of the Fracture.
    * **Giant Hunt (1-4 Players):** A pure boss rush mode. No minions, no objectives—just you and your team against a gauntlet of massive bosses.
    * **Cloud Conduit (4-Player Raid):** The pinnacle of PvE content. A sprawling weekly mission with complex puzzles, unique lieutenants, and a multi-phase raid boss.

## 💻 Technology Stack

This project is being developed with a web-first approach to ensure maximum accessibility across browsers and devices.

* **Client (The Game):** Three.js / JavaScript (ES6+) / HTML5 / CSS3
* **Server (The Backend):** Node.js with WebSockets
* **Database:** MongoDB or PostgreSQL

## 🚀 Project Status & Roadmap

The project is currently in the **early prototyping phase**. Our primary focus is on building a client-side proof of concept to validate the core gameplay loop, controls, and the feel of the Echo System.

For a detailed breakdown of our development plan, please see the documents in the `/blueprint` directory.
