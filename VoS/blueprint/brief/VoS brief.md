# Project Brief: Vessels of Sanctuary
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** July 22, 2025

## 1. Project Summary
**Vessels of Sanctuary** is a free-to-play, team-based Action MOBA designed for the web and mobile platforms. The project's goal is to innovate on the traditional MOBA formula by introducing a dynamic character-swapping mechanic, called the **Echo System**, combined with skill-based action combat controls. The game is set in a unique "tech-fantasy" world shattered by a cataclysm, where players are caught in a tragic time loop.

## 2. Core Pillars
The design and development of this project are guided by three core pillars:

* **Dynamic Swapping Strategy:** The Echo System is the heart of the game. Every design decision, from character kits to itemization, should enhance the strategic depth of controlling two Vessels. Swapping should be integral to combat, survival, and resource management.

* **Skill-Based Action Combat:** Player skill, through direct WASD control, aiming, and dodging, should be the primary determinant of success. The game must feel responsive, fast-paced, and fair.

* **Accessible & Web-First:** The game must be highly accessible, running smoothly in a web browser without a large initial download. This web-first approach dictates a technology stack (HTML5/JS/Node.js) and performance targets that prioritize reach and ease of play.

## 3. Target Audience & Platform
* **Primary Platform:** Web Browser (PC)
* **Secondary Platform:** Mobile (via a dedicated port or responsive web app)
* **Target Audience:** Players of existing MOBAs (e.g., *League of Legends*, *Dota 2*) and Action RPGs (e.g., *Diablo*) who are looking for a new, faster-paced experience with a lower barrier to entry.

## 4. Project Status
The project is currently in the **early prototyping phase**. The immediate focus is on building a client-side proof of concept in Three.js to validate the core gameplay loop, controls, and the feel of the Echo System.
